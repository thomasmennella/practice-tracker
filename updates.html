Updates & Notes
A running record of changes, fixes, and additions
This tool is maintained in whatever time its creator can spare. This page keeps a plain record of what's changed, so you can see how it's evolving. Newest entries are at the top.
August 2026
Meditation timer feature
A timer now lives in the main menu, and on the dashboard under Today. Set any session length, with an optional warm-up of anything from ten seconds upward for settling before you begin. A bowl marks the start of the session, further bowls sound at whatever intervals you set, and a gong closes the sit. Optional background sound: white, pink or brown noise, fan hum, or intermittent tinkling.
  • Every sound is generated in your browser as you listen. There are no audio files, the page makes no network requests, and nothing about your sit is recorded or sent anywhere.
  • Warm-up time is not counted as sitting. When the gong sounds, "Log this sit" carries the session length — not the warm-up — into the sit log for you.
  • Timer settings are remembered on the device you set them on. They are stored separately from your practice log and are not synced.
Two things worth knowing before you rely on it. Keep the screen awake is on by default; leave it on, or disable auto-lock yourself, or the sit may be cut short. And on iPhone the ringer switch normally mutes web audio entirely — there is a setting to override that, but it depends on a Safari feature that not every version has. Run the timer for a minute with your usual setup before trusting it with a real sit.
August 2026
Hindrances logged by degree feature
The five hindrances were previously a row of checkboxes: present or not. Each is now marked None, Minor or Major, with a box for detail on anything you mark. The guidance panel has been rewritten to match.
  • The threshold is functional obstruction, not intensity. The question is not whether something was dramatic but whether it altered your relationship to the object. The canonical similes — the monkey, the man in debt — are the loud end of a spectrum, not the bar for logging.
  • The panel now describes what each hindrance looks like at each degree, including the forms that are easy to miss: torpor that passes for calm, striving that passes for diligence, self-criticism that is still ill will.
Your existing logs are untouched, and continue to display, filter and search exactly as before.
August 2026
A plainer way to record nimitta feature
Recording a nimitta previously meant choosing between parikamma, uggaha and paṭibhāga — which presumes you already know which one you saw. The sit log now offers a second option: a plain description of what the breath actually became by the end of the sit, in ordinary language, with room to write your own. Use whichever is more honest on the day. Entries recorded either way display normally, and existing entries are unchanged.
July 2026
Installable as an app, with offline access feature
The site can now be installed as an app on Android and desktop Chrome (look for the "Install" prompt, or use the browser menu's "Install app"). Installed, it opens full-screen without browser bars and works offline — pages are cached on your device, so it loads instantly and keeps working with no connection. On iPhone/iPad, use Safari's Share → "Add to Home Screen" for the same full-screen experience. As always, your data stays local unless you enable sync.
July 2026
Sutta reading now opens on SuttaCentral fix
In-app sutta reading was removed. The previous version reconstructed sutta text from a segmented source, which could occasionally mis-order passages and distort the meaning — not an acceptable risk for canonical text. Suttas now open directly on SuttaCentral, where the text is authoritative. Searching, read-tracking, and personal reflections all remain in the app.
July 2026
Honest privacy language for sync transparency
The wording around device sync was corrected throughout. A GitHub "secret" Gist is unlisted, not private — anyone with the link can read it, and data is stored unencrypted. The setup and settings pages now say so plainly. Local-only (no sync) remains the fully private default; nothing leaves your device unless you enable sync.
July 2026
Disabling sync now fully stops uploads fix
Previously, turning off sync didn't reliably stop background uploads if credentials were still saved. Sync now honors your choice completely, and disabling it offers to remove the saved token and Gist ID from your device.
July 2026
Contact form added feature
A "Contact Creator" page under Resources lets you send questions, feature requests, or bug reports directly. No reply is guaranteed, but every message is read.
